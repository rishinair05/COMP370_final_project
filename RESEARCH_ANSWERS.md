## Question 2: How much coverage the movie received relative to other movies that came out at a similar time?

### Summary
**Superman (2025) received the most coverage** among the major July 2025 releases, with 361 posts (54.1% of total). Fantastic Four received 23.7% coverage, and Jurassic World received 13.3%.

### Overall Coverage Comparison

| Movie | Total Posts | Percentage |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| **Superman** | 361 posts | **54.1%** |
| Fantastic Four | 158 posts | 23.7% |
| Jurassic World | 89 posts | 13.3% |
| Smurfs | 3 posts | 0.4% |

### Box Office Coverage (Most Discussed Aspect)

Since Box Office is the dominant topic (65% of all posts), the box office coverage directly shows relative interest:

| Movie | Box Office Posts | Percentage of Box Office Posts |
|-------|------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Superman** | 207 posts | **48.1%** |
| Fantastic Four | 135 posts | 31.3% |
| Jurassic World | 94 posts | 21.8% |

### Key Findings
- **Superman dominates**: Receives more than half of all coverage (54.1%)
- **Fantastic Four is second**: 23.7% of total coverage, 31.3% of box office posts
- **Jurassic World is third**: 13.3% of total coverage, 21.8% of box office posts
- **Relative to Superman**: 
  - Fantastic Four receives 67.8% of Superman's coverage
  - Jurassic World receives 54.5% of Superman's coverage

### Conclusion
Superman (2025) received significantly more Reddit coverage than the other major July 2025 releases, particularly in box office discussions where it accounts for nearly half of all posts. This suggests higher community interest and engagement with Superman compared to Fantastic Four and Jurassic World.
